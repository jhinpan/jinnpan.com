not a 4.3x efficiency collapse. The earlier reading assumed both chunks
ran the same form and so attributed the whole gap to the kernel.

The absorbed form is the right choice for decode (one query, many keys:
reading the latent once beats decompressing it). For a 16384-query
prefill chunk it is the wrong side of the trade: 3.4x the FLOPs per pair
to avoid a decompression that would have been amortized over 16384
queries. Chunked prefix cache exists to run the prefix part in the
decompressed form instead -- and it is unavailable on the triton
backend (CHUNKED_PREFIX_CACHE_SUPPORTED_ATTENTION_BACKENDS), which is
why this configuration pays the penalty.
